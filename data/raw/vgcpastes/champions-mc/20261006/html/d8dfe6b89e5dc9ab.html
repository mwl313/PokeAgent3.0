EVs: Farigiraf survives jolly knock T-Tar + adamant Sneasler (if both don't get a max role). Special bulk aimed to survive ZardY Weather Ball. Full SpA modest is a role tho. Sylv Hyper Beam kills the farig. ZardY phys bulk aimed to survive 2x gambit kowtow (non dmg boosted). I primarily ran phys bulk because of Rock Slide/Tomb Sneasler. 1 SpA = jump point. 3 SpD to squeeze out some special bulk (75% Chance to survive non SpA invest Ychu Zap Cannon). Peli survives adamant miracle wood hammer rilla while outspeeding and killing standard adamant Sneasler in tailwind (unaimed special bulk). Arch: adamant CC Sneasler survival. Exca 2 special def to have a higher chance to survive Zchomp earth power. Reasons why I ran scarf mold breaker exca in the first place: I wanted something against Floette/Sylv/Ychu. Mold breaker allows me to hit chompZ with gruond moves. But other potential mons with specific abilities are also threatened by high horse power, although they weren't that popular (Delphox, LucarioZ, Rotom). It was a cool set to run, but realistically: Just run LOrb, modest, sludge, earth, sleep, tect Venu instead. Farigiraf + Zard was my most common lead and it worked really well overall
